# Images

All photographs generated with built-in image_gen for this local implementation. They are illustrative, not photographs of the actual business. None were extracted from the website sketch. No common color filter is applied.

- hero.webp: standalone documentary panorama of ordinary toy-shop aisle, mother and child seen from behind, dense shelves of colorful packages, fluorescent ceiling, sharp detail, no bokeh or overlaid UI. Source 1774×887.
- store.webp: standalone vertical documentary photo of a dense local toy-shop aisle, colorful shelves, LED ceiling, no people, no blur. Source 1161×1355.
- robot.webp: standalone square photo of blue transformer toy held in hand against dark teal fabric, ordinary sharp smartphone photography. Source 1254×1254.
- plush.webp: standalone square photo of blue and pink plush fantasy animals on white shop shelf, cold LED lighting. Source 1254×1254.
- piano.webp: standalone square photo of pink boxed colorful plastic musical piano on gray counter, entire packaging visible. Source 1254×1254.
- games.webp: standalone square photo of generic colorful family board-game boxes on dark wooden shelf, warm light. Source 1254×1254.
- assortment.webp, musical.webp: separate tiles exported from a generated 3×2 photo contact sheet (not a UI sketch). Prompts: overhead counter covered in colorful toy packages; mint/purple musical toys in retail packages on pink backdrop. Each source tile 512×512. Used only in the small Instagram previews.
- logo.png: deliberately neutral placeholder authorised by user, drawn locally; not a recreation of their brand logo.

Brief for all photos: real retail camera framing, mixed backgrounds and lighting, no pastel harmonization, no artificial blur, no website text or controls. Font licences are in ../fonts/OFL.txt; Font Awesome licence is copied by the build into dist/assets/fontawesome/LICENSE.txt.
