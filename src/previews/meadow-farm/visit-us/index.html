    Before you visit

    Do I need to call ahead?

    No appointment or call ahead is needed. Come to the farm store during the posted opening hours.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    The store accepts cash, debit and credit.

    Can we walk around the farm?

    Only the store is open to visitors. The farm grounds, barns and animals are private. Farm tours are not offered.

    Do you sell live chickens, chicks or laying hens?

    No. We sell chicken as food, including whole birds and portions.

    Announcements & Visitor Information

    Biosecurity plan for Meadow Farm

    Why biosecurity? Our farm is currently a “safe zone”. Our animals are healthy and protected, and we want to keep it that way. Visitors coming from other farms, or who have visited other farms, could unintentionally carry diseases or pests that may not affect them but could cause illness, infection or worse for our animals.

    We do not provide farm tours, and our barns and animals are not available to the public.

    Please respect our posted signage.
