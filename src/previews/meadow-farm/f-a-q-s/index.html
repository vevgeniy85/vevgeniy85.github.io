Good to know

A few things before you visit.

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do I need to call ahead?

No appointment or call ahead is needed. Come to the farm store during the posted opening hours.

Do you have eggs?

Farm-fresh brown eggs are part of our store selection.

Can we walk around the farm?

Only the store is open to visitors. The farm grounds, barns and animals are private. Farm tours are not offered.

Which payment methods do you accept?

The store accepts cash, debit and credit.

Is tax added to my purchase?

Tax is charged on taxable products. Ask in the store if you have a question about an item.

Do you sell raw milk?

We do not offer raw milk. The store carries milk supplied by a local dairy.

When is fresh meat available?

Selected fresh beef and chicken are offered on weekends while supplies last. The store also carries frozen meat.

Do you sell live chickens, chicks or laying hens?

No. We sell chicken as food, including whole birds and portions.

Can I visit outside opening hours?

Please visit during our published store hours. We are closed on Mondays.

Where does your honey come from?

Our farm has its own apiary. The bees forage on wildflowers across the farm.

Are public washrooms available?

There are no public washrooms on the property.

Only the store is open to visitors. Barns, animals and surrounding property are private. Please stay in designated areas and respect posted signs.
